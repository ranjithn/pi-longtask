# Approved decisions

Complete these entries from the actual repository and preflight audit before approving.

## Scope and dependencies
Record selected WPs, requirements, compatibility constraints and intentional non-goals.

## Test environment
Record safe database/schema names, fixture strategy, service endpoints, approved side effects and any restrictions. Do not include credentials or personal data.

## Browser acceptance
Record the frontend worktree launch method, test port, Playwright scenarios and test accounts. Confirm that tests exercise the new worktree rather than a stale base-checkout server.

## Architecture decisions
Record answers to every material preflight question and relevant trade-offs.

## Blocking policy
When execution needs an unresolved owner decision or unavailable infrastructure, stop with evidence rather than guessing, weakening checks or widening scope.
